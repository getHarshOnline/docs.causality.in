# Contributing to MONAD

Contributors will propose changes to this repository after accepting the contributor agreement below, and each change is held to the engineering standard that follows. MONAD is not accepting outside contributions yet: the agreement and its acceptance mechanism need legal review before public contribution intake opens.

## Contribution rights

Contributors retain copyright in their original contributions. Before RTEPL accepts a contribution, the contributor must accept the separate [Contributor License Agreement](CLA.md). That agreement gives RawThoughts Enterprises Private Limited permanent project-use, distribution, sublicensing, and relicensing rights, including the right to offer the combined project under commercial terms without later renegotiation or approval.

A Developer Certificate of Origin is not a substitute for this agreement. Accepted contributions follow the outbound licence of their owning source home:

- original kernel, Lean formalization and paper work uses `AGPL-3.0-or-later`;
- third-party material, including the Lean toolchain and mathlib, retains its applicable licence.

## Engineering standard

A contribution must preserve the repository's existing ownership boundaries and describe only what exists. Build and check it as [the getting-started guide](docs/getting-started.md) and [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md) describe: Lean owns the definitions and proofs, and the paper and the Rust kernel follow them. Preserve third-party provenance and applicable notices.

## Attribution

Harsh Joshi is the author and originator. RawThoughts Enterprises Private Limited owns the project, its distribution, the collective codebase and the commercial licensing authority. Contributors are acknowledged for accepted work; a patch does not automatically change the canonical author entry in `CITATION.cff`.

Use only project-owned or properly licensed names, imagery and other material. Do not imply endorsement by another project or rights holder.
