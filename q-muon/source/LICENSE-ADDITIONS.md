<a id="fork&#45;additions"></a>

Q\-MUON adds a [draft paper](/q-muon/source/paper/Q-MUON-tex), its bibliography and its [quantum unit definitions](/q-muon/source/paper/shared/q-muon/definitions-tex)\. Its [kernel scaffold](/q-muon/source/kernel/src/lib-rs) re\-exports the pinned MONAD kernel\.

The [protocol header](/q-muon/source/protos/muon/unit-proto) and classical unit definitions come from [MUON](https://github.com/getHarshOnline/MUON)\. The formal definitions, paper Makefile and validation script come from [MONAD](https://github.com/getHarshOnline/MONAD)\. These copies keep their existing grants and notices\.
