Q\-MUON inherits material from MUON; its present quantum\-specific additions include [the paper](/q-muon/source/paper/Q-MUON-tex) and [its definitions](/q-muon/source/paper/shared/q-muon/definitions-tex), while the protocol header is inherited from MUON\.

Existing grants and notices remain in force, including the [AGPL](/q-muon/source/LICENSE) terms permitting commercial use; the rights review must establish applicable grants, attribution and RTEPL’s rights before any alternative licence, which requires a separate written agreement as the [README](/q-muon/repository#licence) states\.
